## Research & product strategy

### DeepLoc 2.0 Plant Protein Benchmark

`Bioinformatics · Model Evaluation · UniProt · Error Analysis`

A bioinformatics evaluation of how DeepLoc 2.0 generalizes to newer experimentally annotated plant proteins.

Curated a 163-protein temporal holdout from UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot, standardized nine localization labels, and reported 69.33% top-1 accuracy with class-level error analysis (macro-F1 0.514).

_Team project · Completed · report-backed._

`Market Research · Python · Product Strategy · Vue · CMAU`

A research-driven education product concept that starts with research-capability development before connecting students to international study pathways.

Combined 194 target survey responses, reliability/validity checks, regression and public-text analysis with a Vue prototype covering assessment, results, services, community, school selection, and dashboard flows.
